GCSE Key Concepts: Carbon & Climate

Essential revision facts linked directly to the decisions in the game.

Chemistry Paper 2 • Geography Paper 1
🌡️

Greenhouse Effect

Short-wavelength UV radiation from the Sun passes through the atmosphere and warms Earth. Earth radiates long-wavelength infrared back, which gases like CO₂, CH₄ (methane), and H₂O absorb and re-radiate.

👣

Carbon Footprint (CO₂e)

The total amount of carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases emitted over the full life cycle of a product, service, or event—measured in CO₂ equivalents (CO₂e) because methane traps ~28× more heat than CO₂.

⚡

UK Energy Mix

Wind and solar are clean renewables, but they are intermittent (dependent on weather). When wind drops during evening peak demand, natural gas turbines ramp up quickly—releasing CO₂.

♻️

Life Cycle Assessments

GCSE Chemistry evaluates 4 stages: 1) Extracting raw materials, 2) Manufacturing & packaging, 3) Use & operation, 4) Disposal. Buying second-hand clothes skips stages 1 and 2!

How to Publish This Game on GitHub Pages (github.io)

index.html

Because this entire website and game is built into a single HTML file, you can host it live on your own username.github.io link in under 2 minutes:

STEP 01

Create a GitHub Repo

Log into github.com, click New Repository, name it carbon-smart-gcse, set it to Public, and click Create.

STEP 02

Upload as index.html

Save this file as index.html, click Add file → Upload files in your repo, drag it in, and commit changes.

STEP 03

Turn On GitHub Pages

Go to Settings → Pages, select Deploy from a branch (main), hit Save, and your live .github.io URL appears!
